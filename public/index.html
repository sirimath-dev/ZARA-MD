05 — HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about connecting and managing Queen Zara.

How do I connect Queen Zara?

Generate a pairing code, open WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link with phone number, then enter your code.

How do I open the dashboard?

Click the Dashboard button and sign in using the credentials supported by your backend.

Where can I get my dashboard password?

Follow the password delivery process configured in your bot backend.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset method configured for your bot account.

Can I manage bot settings?

Yes. Open the settings dashboard to manage the options supported by your backend.

How can I disconnect my WhatsApp?

You can remove the linked device through WhatsApp → Linked Devices.
