Quick answers

How do I connect?

Generate a code in the Connect section, then open WhatsApp, Linked devices, Link with phone number and enter it.

How do I open the panel?

Go to the settings page and log in with your number and password. The password is sent to your own WhatsApp chat when you connect. Forgot it? Send .resetpass from the bot's own chat.

Why do bot messages show “Forwarded”?

If Forward Tag is on, bot messages are sent in a forwarded channel style.

Where does view once media go?

To your own chat or the same chat, depending on “Send notices to”. With emoji reply, it always goes to your own chat. The bot must be online when the view once message arrives.

How do I recover a view once with an emoji?

Turn on “View once emoji reply” in Protection. Then reply to a view once message with an emoji and the bot sends the photo or video to your own chat.

Can anti delete work only in private chats or only in groups?

Yes. In Protection you can choose All chats, Private only or Groups only, separately for anti delete and anti edit.

How do I save someone's status?

Turn on “Status forward” to get every new status in your own chat, or reply to a status with .save (or an emoji, if “Save by reply” is on) and it is sent to your own chat.

How do scheduled messages and statuses work?

Open the Schedule tab, choose a message or status, pick a time and add it. You can repeat it daily. Scheduled statuses go to your own account and contacts the bot has seen since it started.

How do I remove the bot?

Use “Disconnect bot” in the panel, or remove the linked device in WhatsApp. Your stored session is deleted.
